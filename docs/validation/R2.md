# R2 ranged packages — 13 October 2026

Branch `r2-ranged-packages`, based on updated main `cb7c2cd` (including merged
R1.5a, neutral/DPS presentation and exact magic-gap labels). Plugin **0.7.0**,
knowledge **r2-1**, catalogue schema **9**, ranged **ranged-1**, magic
**powered-magic-2**, ranking **pursuit-1** and local preference schema **2**.
Implementation is ready for review; actual-client acceptance remains pending.

An account with Bowfa keeps its crystal armour. Masori substitution can lose DPS
and is deferred for that reference. Tbow remains a separate long-term specialist:
its neutral Magic-0 result can lose damage while its reviewed Zilyana package
improves it. Owning tbow removes that acquisition without erasing Bowfa/light-attack
alternatives. These are contextual comparisons, never one universal grind order.

## Inputs, priority and paths

Magic and ranged references have independent session-only corrections, confirmation
and target selection. Ranged automatically compares observed compatible packages,
including crystal versus ordinary armour, complete Void, off-hands and supplied
ammunition. Manual ownership cannot enter a usable baseline. Unknown equipped gear
with no reviewed replacement blocks numbers rather than becoming zero. Dart type
is an explicit proposed internal load; observed darts do not prove the actual load.
Candidate-only ammunition is clearly identified when the account has no observed
compatible supply. Confirmed references are still estimates, not measurements.

No prayer/boost and Rapid are default ranged assumptions. Focused controls support
Accurate/Longrange, a full ranging-potion scenario, Eagle Eye, Deadeye and Rigour.
Unlock/Prayer/Defence conditions are revalidated. Task assumptions only apply to
reviewed eligible boss contexts with an imbued helmet. Levels, containers, slots,
quantities, manual facts and unlock changes invalidate confirmation. Character,
profile and lifecycle clears discard both families and their corrections; stale
callbacks cannot edit the new session. No reference/observation persistence was added.

Weapon Value leads with DPS, and accessories retain max hit before the secondary
metrics. Separate encounter examples disclose their own complete default proposals,
sources and assumptions. Positive specialist value may retain a long-term outcome
despite a neutral loss; it cannot enter a universal cross-target numerical tie or
override remaining effort. Context proposals are reused within each immutable
evaluation, avoiding repeated package searches for every accessory.

Paths distinguish Bowfa self-singing **100 shards + 82 Crafting/Smithing** from
**150-shard NPC singing**. Crystal pieces likewise have exclusive self/NPC branches,
including the extra 60-shard fee. Quantities use the existing conservative largest
read container count; a moved stack is never added twice. A confirmed seed/required
seed quantity, fang, vestige or completed intermediate removes the corresponding
acquisition stage and activity vote, while remaining resources/skills stay separate.
Inactive/empty outcomes suppress duplicate acquisition but do not become supplied
reference weapons. No probability or hour estimate was fabricated for these routes.

The toxic route uses the reviewed **78 Fletching** requirement (raised from 53 in
May 2024), chisel, tanzanite fang and separate darts/scales. Rosewood uses 84
Fletching, two rosewood logs and a squid beak, with rune darts permitted by the
July 2026 update. Anguish uses a **necklace** mould and 92 Crafting, retaining
onyx/fusion/gem/enchantment work and intermediate bypasses. Rupture identifies the
Blood Moon quest, stomach loot versus pet-egg choice, 84 Crafting/chisel etching,
bank-side combination and 90 Hitpoints equip requirement. Venator checks the
Leviathan kill, Peer instruction, icons, blood runes, three ingots and furnace/mould.
Assembler distinguishes an owned head from an unobserved lifetime kill-50 guarantee;
it cannot promise a future guarantee for an already-used/lost head. Quiver challenge
and raid time remain qualitative. Blood Moon Rises and Dragon Slayer II are read
through supported public `Quest.getState`, with coalesced progress refresh.

Schema 9 adds bounded typed ranged stats/ammo/family/set metadata and reviewed
ranged target fields, plus path-stage acquisition markers. Source dependencies
cover these items/models/contexts/routes/resources. Unknown or malformed hands,
ammo defaults, defence types, target inputs and evidence fail closed. Stable
preference IDs are retained; no preference migration or storage expansion is needed.

passes **186 tests with zero failures/errors/skips**. All
earlier magic oracle and lifecycle regressions remain; historical magic-only

no GPL code or equipment dataset was copied into the BSD plugin. An initial harness
name-case mismatch prevented reference crystal recognition and was corrected before
freezing results; matching that faulty harness was never used as acceptance.

successful values below 3; misses remain 0. No uniform 3-to-max damage shortcut.
