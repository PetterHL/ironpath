## Independent mechanics checks

For visible Magic L, seas/swamp/Sang/Eye/Shadow base hits are `floor(L/3)-5`, `floor(L/3)-2`, `floor(L/3)`, `floor(L/3)-6`, `floor(L/3)+1`. Attack intervals are 4/4/4/3/5 ticks. Effective accuracy is `floor(L × prayer accuracy multiplier) + style bonus + 8`. Gear damage uses tenth-percent units, retaining regular/imbued seers' 0.2%/0.5%.

Shadow multiplies total equipment attack, including its own +35, and gear damage by three outside ToA. Its ToA variant uses four, with gear damage capped to 100% before prayer damage is added. Two-handed replacement removes the off-hand. Non-elemental powered attacks receive no wind weakness. Ordinary Dharok's defence roll is 530; Graardor's is `(80+9) × (298+64) = 32218`.

Sang's implemented July update adds 8 damage with probability 1/5 on successful attacks. An initial reference disagreement exposed its omission. The current Wiki/holy variant and official Jagex article confirm the proc. For pre-proc max M, the successful-hit mean is `M/2 + 1.6 + 0.8/(M+1)` because only non-proc zeros receive the final floor to one; reported maximum is M+8. Misses stay zero. Healing is not translated into trips. Ordinary staves' successful mean remains `M/2 + 1/(M+1)`.

Hand example: 99 Magic, saturated heart, Augury, Accurate, Shadow, Ancestral hat/top/bottom, imbued cape, occult, tormented, eternal and seers (i), empty ammo/off-hand. Visible Magic 112; equipment attack 161 × 3 = 483; effective accuracy 151; attack roll 82597. Gear damage 22.5% × 3 + prayer 4% = 71.5%; max hit 65. Graardor hit chance is `1 - 32220/(2 × 82598)` and theoretical DPS is that chance times `(65/2 + 1/66) / 3`.

The hypothetical ToA cap hand case uses 26% gear, four-times multiplication capped at 100%, then Augury's 4%; 99-Magic Shadow max is 69. **This is a mechanic check, not a shipped ToA target estimate.** ToA phase/raid/path/party scaling stays unsupported and the service refuses every metric rather than choosing a default invocation.

Source revisions and current-review provenance are in the [bundled register](../../src/main/resources/com/ironpath/catalog.json). The official 22 July article is linked evidence with its retrieved SHA-256; no article text is distributed. No calculator implementation, bulk equipment dataset, upstream test corpus, guide prose or game artwork was imported.

## Reproduction and actual results

Use the normal JDK 11 project command:

```sh
./gradlew test build --no-daemon
```

The managed environment used temporary Temurin 11.0.32.1, supported network permission, the session proxy and system CA trust as in the existing historical build notes. RuneLite resolves to 1.13.1. **124 tests pass, zero failures/errors/skips.** Non-fatal Fontconfig cache and Gradle deprecation warnings remain. `git diff --check` passes.

The old 234-case original P4a CSV still passes through shared arithmetic. The new 540-case [CSV](../../src/test/resources/com/ironpath/r1-oracle.csv) checks whole max hits and integer rolls exactly, hit chance within `1e-12`, and DPS within `1e-10`. It spans three Magic levels, five powered staff families, three complete armour packages, two targets, both styles and three prayer/boost scenarios. All rows match after the Sang correction. The corpus contains original synthetic inputs and reference outputs, not player data or upstream datasets.

## Remaining boundaries and live checks

ToA scaling, one-handed Confliction accuracy history, Brimstone accuracy procs, ancient/elemental spells and specialist target effects remain explicit model gaps. Confliction's flat stats are supported with Shadow; one-handed history and Brimstone retain max hit while withholding accuracy/DPS. Healing, special-energy opportunity cost, resources, expected kill time and trip performance are not inferred from DPS.

Container counts are planning evidence. The largest observed container count is a conservative lower bound, not a sum across potentially stale observations or a live supply guarantee. Unknown/insufficient readings cannot complete resource steps. Unsupported gear can be corrected or explicitly excluded from a reference, with that assumption visible. Corrected references remain scenarios, not universal account optimisation.

Actual RuneLite 100%, 125%, 150% and 200% scaling; sprite rendering; real bank quantities/refresh; quest/unlock observations; restart/account/profile transitions; reference corrections; and a player finding useful upgrades remain **pending**. Preserve all historical pending checks. No game client was launched, no live account data was collected, no Plugin Hub publication occurred, and comprehensive coverage is not claimed.
