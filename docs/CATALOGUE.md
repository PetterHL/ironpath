## How to read and maintain this inventory

**D** = discovered candidate; name/family cross-checked against reference item data or existing evidence, but current route/mechanics need item-level review. **R** = researched candidate with relevant mechanics/release evidence inspected; not an implemented validated recommendation. **V** = validated recommendation in the recorded implementation increment for its specific contexts, with real-client validation still pending. **Q0** = quantitative support only within P4a's documented trident/Dharok contract. **—** = no shipped quantitative model. A proposed model code is a backlog mapping, never a claim of calculation support.

“BiS candidate”, “specialist” and “bridge” below describe the role to test. They do not certify best performance for every encounter. Marginal value must be evaluated against the account's strongest supported owned alternative, with a defined target. Each armour piece, prayer, component route and meaningful variant receives a separate runtime record when validated, even where a family is grouped here for readability. Do not count cosmetics as upgrades or assume a complete set from one piece.

The **D** inventory is an original research checklist: its baseline/use/defer columns are hypotheses and verification questions, not facts approved for runtime. Discovery used the locally inspected [Wiki calculator item names/slots at 89c3e25](https://github.com/weirdgloop/osrs-dps-calc/tree/89c3e25b344aea90d0189746e4b5f73dde0f0383/cdn/json), prior pinned project evidence and indexed Wiki item/strategy pages. No upstream data table, stats corpus or artwork is imported. Item links below are review targets; a link alone is not a claim that its full current page was accessible. Direct Wiki access was blocked in that 12 October discovery session and indexed versions can be stale. The 13 October relationship review retrieved full current pages and pins their revisions separately; it does not retroactively promote unrelated rows. Exact IDs, levels, drop rates, charges, post-release changes and source rights still require promotion checks.

## Coverage unit and promotion criteria

A record needs stable family and exact-form IDs; combat style/slot/role; released/future status; replaced and complementary items; contextual BiS/significant/specialist/marginal classification; applicability/owned alternatives; expected mechanism of gain; supported metrics and excluded mechanics; acquisition DAG; hard versus recommended requirements; equip versus acquisition distinction; upkeep/effort assumptions; defer reasons; source revisions and review date; model dependencies; account fixtures; and live-validation status.

Promote D → R with inspected item/mechanic/acquisition evidence and an original conditional recommendation. Promote R → V only after route/variant validation and account-quality checks, including owned-superior and unknown-ownership counterexamples. Add a Q capability per **context and metric**, with independent numerical tests. A recently released BiS item must not wait for a universal simulator to receive a properly qualified qualitative V entry; its quantitative gap remains scheduled. Unsupported stats/variants remain explicit, never zero-filled.

For each content wave report: all named outcomes accounted for as V, R/D with an explicit gap, or excluded with a reason; recognition versus recommendation versus calculation coverage; encounter/slot omissions; stale/withdrawn records. “Four of four implemented” says nothing about endgame completeness. Future announcements do not inflate released coverage.
